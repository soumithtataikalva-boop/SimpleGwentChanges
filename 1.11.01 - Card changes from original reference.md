# Card changes: original reference → 1.11.01

Sources: `old/Original reference copy.xml` and `1.11.01 - Card index cleanup.xml`.

Cards are matched by internal name, so Geralt and Ciri are compared with their original definitions despite their moved indices. All changed attributes and flag lists are included; formatting and unchanged fields are omitted.

Excluded: regular and summoned Regis (`emiel`, `emiel_summoned`), regular and summoned Olgierd (`olgierd`, `olgierd_summoned`), and the original GOG entries (`geraltGOG`, `ciriGOG`).

88 changed cards are documented. Faction headings use the 1.11.01 faction; duplicate copies have separate entries. “Muster / summon group” is the XML effect `EFFECT_SUMMON_CLONES`.
