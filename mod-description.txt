[size=4][b]Description[/b][/size]

[size=3]Reworks The Witcher 3's Gwent through changes to card strength, factions, rows and abilities, alongside revised opponent decks and supporting item and shop definitions.

The aim is to make overlooked cards more useful, give factions more varied options, and introduce new combinations while keeping the familiar Gwent rules. Some choices also reflect the characters and creatures behind the cards.

Regis and Olgierd gain summoned follow-up forms. Geralt summons Roach, Letho becomes Neutral, and Gaunter O'Dimm and Darkness join Monsters. Several Heroes become regular units, opening them up to effects they previously ignored.[/size]

[size=4][b]Choose Your Version[/b][/size]

[size=3][b]SGR — Standalone[/b]
For use without the BIA Gwent additions. Includes the shared card changes and revised opponent decks, with standalone-compatible card choices.

[b]SGRBIA — BIA-Compatible[/b]
Uses the same core changes and incorporates the BIA Gwent card and item definitions: Witch Hunter, Ballista Officer, a second Siege Tower, and two additional Blue Stripes Commando variants. Ballista Officer belongs to Northern Realms in this version. Existing card variants also use BIA's distinct item bindings and artwork references.

The BIA-compatible version still requires the relevant BIA assets and reward functionality. Install only one version of this mod.[/size]

[size=4][b]Opponent Decks[/b][/size]

[size=3]Opponent decks have been revised alongside the player-facing card changes:
[list]
[*]All nonempty static opponent decks contain at least 22 creature cards. Empty faction templates are preserved.
[*]Easy decks favor simpler combinations; normal and hard decks receive more supporting cards and fuller synergies.
[*]Muster and Tight Bond cards have partners or additional copies available in their static or dynamic deck definitions.
[*]Repeated creatures have been reviewed, with exceptions where duplicates are needed for their abilities. No individual card exceeds three copies in a static deck.
[*]Weather, Decoy, Commander's Horn and Scorch are capped at three copies each in static decks.
[*]Card choices vary between opponents and difficulty levels, using faction-compatible units and Neutral cards where appropriate.
[/list]
These revisions change opponent deck composition and difficulty. Difficulty and balance have not yet been verified through a full in-game playthrough.[/size]

[size=4][b]Items And Shops[/b][/size]

[size=3]Inventory icons and faction tags have been updated to reflect the changed cards, including Letho, Gaunter O'Dimm, Darkness and Schirrú. Collection-limit definitions and shop card selections are included. The BIA-compatible version includes the additional BIA Gwent item definitions and uses the appropriate card variants in shops.[/size]

[size=4][b]Installation[/b][/size]

[size=3]Choose the standalone or BIA-compatible release, then install the packaged mod with Witcher Mod Manager, or extract its mod folder into your Witcher 3 installation's [b]Mods[/b] directory.

For the BIA-compatible release, keep the required BIA assets and reward scripts available. Conflicting BIA Gwent XML definitions must be suppressed or overridden by this version; do not load both competing sets of definitions unchanged.

Other mods that edit the same Gwent card, item, opponent-deck or shop definitions may conflict. Resolve these conflicts before playing.[/size]
